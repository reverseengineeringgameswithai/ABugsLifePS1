## Primary role: the player damage handler (2026-08-01)

**This function is the player damage handler.** Every damage source funnels through
it — 6 `j`/`jal` callers, enumerated statically over the whole resident image:
`0x800496B0`, `0x80049740`, `0x80049868` (player_vertical_update), `0x8004B4A0`
(actor_player_contact_resolver), `0x80058A48` (contact_pickup_walker), `0x8005C9F8`
(seed_plant_mine_handler).

Structure:

- `0x8004A7A4` reads the power slot `0x800A657E`; `0x8004A7B4 bne` **routes** to the
  power-interception block. What suppresses the health write is register **`s1`**
  being cleared (`0x8004A818` / `0x8004A800`), tested by `0x8004A828 beq s1,zero`.
- `0x8004A7E8 bgez` splits invincibility (non-negative, quota untouched) from the
  bubble (negative, quota `+1` at `0x8004A808`, unless already staggered).
- `0x8004A888 sh v1,0x6594(at)` is the health decrement — the **unique damage-side
  decrement among 6 statically resolved resident writers** of `0x800A6594`. The earlier
  "only 4 instructions" count was wrong because a fixed-form scan missed two aliased
  zero-offset stores. This resolved set is not an absolute closure: the address census
  retains unresolved-base accesses and indirect calls.
- `0x8004A890 bgez` on the **new** health selects the **death branch**: sfx 73, sound
  12, then `0x8004A8B4 sh 2,0x800823A0`, `0x80082470 := 60`, stagger `:= -45`.

See [[SYS_player_power_slot]] for the full model.

### Two corrections to the migrated Purpose paragraph above

1. **`0x800A6594` is the player's TRUE HEALTH, not a "level-exit counter."** Runtime
   evidence: in `movies/B08_movie.json` it decrements by exactly 1 at `0x8004A888` on
   each landed hit (4→3 at the frame the operator recorded being hit), reaches 0/negative
   after forced hits, and the respawn handler restores it to 4. The `bltz` at
   `0x8004F0E0` that the paragraph cites is an **alive check** guarding a call to
   [[FUNC_8004ABF4]], not an exit gate. This matches the Phase-2 binding
   (true health `s16 @ 0x800A6594`).
2. **The `[0x800823a0] = 2` store at `0x8004A8B4` is on the DEATH branch**, reached
   only when the health decrement drives health negative. So the census name
   `resident_level_transition_initiator` describes a real but **secondary** effect of
   one branch, not the function's role. An overlay verb calling this function to
   trigger a transition would be doing so *by killing the player*; the xref claim in
   the paragraph above is not contradicted by this work (overlay code was outside the
   resident-image scan), but its interpretation of the function is.

# Whole-Game Mechanics Baseline

**Navigation:** [[Game Solving Roadmap - From Reconstruction to Reliable Modding|Roadmap hub]] · [[Phase 1 - Establish the Whole-Game Mechanics and Campaign Baseline|Owning phase]]

## Purpose

Define the complete player-facing and engine-facing surface of the pristine PlayStation
game before claiming that the game is solved. This baseline prevents recent MOD work,
LEVEL01 familiarity, or reconstructed-byte percentages from determining what receives
attention.

This is a planning and audit note, not Canon. External descriptions identify behaviors
that must exist in the retail game; they do not establish code addresses or internal
semantics. Only pristine-image static/runtime evidence may promote those claims into
Canon.

## Status vocabulary

- **Solved:** satisfies every item in the roadmap's strict subsystem Definition of
  Done. No player-facing mechanic is granted this status merely because one function,
  table, or outcome is confirmed.
- **Partial:** local static/runtime evidence confirms an important slice, but the full
  behavior, lifecycle, campaign breadth, or oracle is incomplete.
- **Unresolved:** the mechanic is known to exist, but its implementation and behavioral
  contract are not adequately mapped.
- **External lead:** reported by the manual or campaign documentation but not yet
  confirmed against the verified pristine PS1 image.
- **Not applicable:** the manual explicitly assigns no function, such as Circle in the
  default PlayStation controls; this still requires an input-path negative control.

At baseline creation (2026-07-11), **no gameplay row below met the strict Solved
definition**. Several rows contained strong confirmed slices, but that is not
whole-mechanic closure.

**That statement still holds.** On 2026-08-01 *Temporary invincibility* and *Bubble
shield* were promoted to Solved and then **retracted to Partial the same day** after an
independent review measured them against the strict Definition of Done above. The
mechanism they share — a single sign-multiplexed halfword — is confirmed and reproducible
from the pristine EXE ([[SYS_player_power_slot]]), but the evidence grade was not closure
grade.

Work since the retraction has met DoD items 1 and 9 — both rows now have a
deterministic cold-boot oracle asserted by a committed runner, and their fixtures are
centrally hash-pinned — and substantially advanced items 2 and 6 with an alias-aware
frontier census, a first-ever overlay census, and the first natural collection outside
one stage. They remain **Partial** because items 4, 7 and 8 are open and the frontier
residual is non-empty. This is the distinction the vocabulary above exists to protect:
**a confirmed mechanism is not a solved subsystem**, and neither is a well-evidenced
one until every item is satisfied. Per-item status and closure path in
[[Phase 4 - Solve Every Gameplay and Engine Subsystem]].

## G. Whole-game state and lifecycle dimensions

Every mechanic above must be tested across the applicable dimensions rather than only
at its easiest trigger:

- Cold boot, load, first use, repeated use, exhaustion/capacity, retirement, and reuse.
- Pause/status, damage, death, respawn-anchor restore, life loss, game over, and continue.
- Level exit, objective completion, results, save, reload, and scene/overlay transition.
- Camera modes, animation states, render double buffering, audio start/stop, and effects.
- Native combinations: seed carrying while damaged, plant powers while jumping,
  projectiles during pause/transition, enemies interacting with hazards/plants, and
  objective state while optional success tokens change.
- Campaign breadth: representative normal, traversal-special, boss, chase, city,
  interior, exterior, and bonus stages.

## Baseline conclusion

The project has many strong primitives and confirmed implementation slices, but the
whole pristine game is not yet semantically closed. The immediate gap is not discovering
whether the game has more mechanics; the retail surface is now bounded well enough to
organize the solve. Phase 1 must turn every row into a tracked campaign item, and Phases
2-6 must supply the original oracle, semantic ownership, subsystem closure,
cross-system integration, and matching/source coverage required to change a status to
Solved.
